# AI PDF Filler beta: naming and search evidence

Research date: 29 September 2026. The owner chose **AI PDF Filler**, displayed with a separate **Beta** badge, and the route `/ai-pdf-filler/`. The intent is filling and signing an existing PDF form, especially an old scanned or unfillable PDF, rather than general-purpose web forms. English and Hebrew are the first accuracy-evaluation languages; launch SEO and UI can remain English initially.

## Three additional name choices

These are alternatives to the earlier AI Form Filler, AI PDF, and AI Fill & Sign suggestions.

| Name | Evidence and tradeoff |
| --- | --- |
| **AI PDF Filler** | Recommended public name, with a separate **Beta** badge. The words were tested directly in Trends; the complete-week mean was 48.23. Clearly identifies the document and job, although the page should explicitly say form filling. |
| **PDF Autofill** | The tested words had a mean of 42.48. Concise and describes the result; needs an AI/Beta descriptor to distinguish assistance from ordinary saved-data autofill. |
| **Smart Fill & Sign** | A branding inference from the tested `fill and sign pdf` term (49.31), which describes the complete workflow. The exact proposed name was **not measured**, so no search demand is claimed for it. |

The recommendation combines search evidence with product fit. It is not a declaration that AI PDF Filler has the largest current market or is easiest to rank for.

### Interpretation limits

Google Trends is sampled, normalized relative interest on a 0–100 scale, not monthly keyword volume. Zeros can reflect insufficient data. Statistical noise can affect low-interest queries; changes of geography, period, or term set alter the comparison context. No keyword difficulty, CPC, traffic estimate, or search-result rank has been established. [Google's methodology](https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4365533?hl=en).

Unquoted multiword inputs can match searches containing the words in different orders, with additional words. Thus “tested directly” refers to the entered words, not a measured exact phrase or exact public brand. This comparison also does not aggregate Hebrew translations, spelling variants, or synonyms. [Google's search-term rules](https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4359582?hl=en).

## Page and route recommendation

- Confirmed public route: **`/ai-pdf-filler/`**. Internal tool folder: `ai-pdf-filler`. This report records the decision; it does not implement the route.
- Title: **AI PDF Form Filler (Beta) – Fill & Sign | PDkef**.
- H1: **Fill PDF forms with AI**; separate Beta badge.
- Description, once the workflow is available: **Fill scanned and digital PDF forms with AI. Review answers, adjust their placement, then sign and download. Manual editing is always available.**
- Primary intent: fill an existing PDF form with AI. Supporting language: fill scanned PDF forms, fill unfillable PDF forms, PDF autofill, fill and sign PDF, editable answers, English and Hebrew PDF forms. General web-form builders and website autofill are outside the product scope; the `ai form filler` comparison term was a naming contrast, not the target category.

Keep the current manual sign page focused on manual fill-and-sign intent. Cross-link both pages with clear eligibility, provider availability, and privacy information. The AI page should demonstrate an actual filled document, exact answer placement, correction, and export. Avoid a broad “AI PDF” promise: [Adobe's PDF AI page](https://www.adobe.com/in/acrobat/online/pdf-ai.html) focuses on analysis, explanation, and summarization, which is a different task.

Product-category wording is corroborated by primary competitor pages, not by measured rankings: [pdf.net](https://pdf.net/pdf-form-filler) describes source-document filling of flat/scanned PDFs with review and manual corrections; [Instafill](https://instafill.ai/) uses AI Form Filler and describes field extraction, source matching, and a visual editor. Treat their claims as vendor positioning, not verified accuracy benchmarks.

Do not claim “free unlimited AI,” universal account compatibility, access to ChatGPT memory, perfect positioning, or that all processing stays on-device. Publish only capabilities demonstrated by the beta. Before provider feasibility is proven, use an honest beta-preview description and avoid indexing a nonfunctional tool as an available service. Index a useful public beta page once its workflow and eligibility are clear. Measure resulting queries and conversion with available Search Console data after launch; revisit the name if observed demand differs.
