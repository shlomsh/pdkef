# PE-15 · A photographer's shadow, and whether HDR helps

*Opened 2026-11-04* from Shlomi: some photos have the shadow of the person taking them; can HDR photos
help get a shadow-free result? Related: [SCAN-19](SCAN-19.md) found no licence-clean learned deshadow model.

## What we know and what we do not

- A learned deshadower is out: DocShadow (MIT code) has no weights licence, an unstated training-data
  licence and is about 118 MB and its ONNX export takes fixed input sizes; the others fail the licence rule.
- `filters.ts`'s Document look already divides each pixel by the paper level around it (blurred block
  grid, `PAPER_REACH` 8 blocks), so soft shading and faint folds are partly handled. **Not measured** on a
  hard-edged shadow, where the shadow edge is sharper than that grid. [unverified]
- HDR, reasoned and not measured [unverified]: a shadow is a change in light, not a lack of dynamic range.
  A merged exposure helps the dark side of the edge (less noise, nothing clipped on the bright side) but the
  edge stays, so HDR is an input improvement for the shading correction, not a fix by itself. Phone camera
  apps already merge frames before saving a JPEG or HEIC. In the browser the live camera cannot bracket
  (no exposure control on iOS Safari; `ImageCapture` exposure and torch only on Chromium for Android), and
  a decoded canvas does not expose a gain map. Where the page is shot under flash or torch the shadow mostly
  disappears, which is the cheapest remedy: check the torch constraint in `useCamera.ts`.

## Outcome

A measured answer, from a few of Shlomi's own shadowed photos kept in a scratchpad: how far the Document
look gets, what an edge-preserving paper-level estimate (large median or masked closing, then divide)
adds, whether the torch helps, and whether HDR input changes anything.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Shlomi supplies 3-5 shadowed photos (private; never in the repo).
- [ ] Before and after on each, scored by residual brightness step across the shadow edge on blank paper.
- [ ] Decision written here: classical estimate adopted or not, torch advice, HDR yes or no with the evidence.
- [ ] Flat, evenly lit pages come out byte-identical or visibly unchanged.

## First real evidence (2026-11-04, SCAN-44's fresh scans)

Two pages of a real scan carry a strong cast shadow and a warm and cool colour split in the Original look. The
Document look removes both on those pages, read by eye, so the remaining question is the Original look only.
