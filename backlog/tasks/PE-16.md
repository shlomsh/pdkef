# PE-16 · Creases and wrinkles in phone photos

*Opened 2026-11-04.* **Track: phone photo of a page** (not book scans; see the handoff's two tracks). From
[SCAN-19](SCAN-19.md): on Shlomi's folded letters (IMG_4757, IMG_4758) the shipped dewarp finds no bend, and no
open-source package or permissive model does better (research 2026-11-04: every dewarper fits one smooth sheet
from long text rows; only Unfolder targets folds and has no code).

## Outcome

A sheet folded in halves or thirds is treated as facets: a fold finder (shading dip plus the kink of text rows
and rules across it) splits the corner-flattened page into pieces, each gets its own lean and bend (the book
result: one rotation per piece reaches the noise floor), and the pieces are joined in one resample. Wrinkles and
curl keep the smooth fit. Pages with no rows or rules are left to their corners.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Scored on the synthetic warped-page corpus (creases, thirds, cross, curl, wrinkles; text, table, sparse and
  blank pages) against the shipped fit: recovery per case is reported here and the finder beats the shipped fit on
  every crease case that has text or rules, without harming the control (identity) pages or the flat photos.
- [ ] IMG_4757 and IMG_4758: the kink across the fold is measured before and after (before: -0.5 and -2.3 deg).
- [ ] Pure function with synthetic unit tests; on a page with no detectable fold the plan is the plain page.

## Baseline: the shipped fit on the synthetic corpus (measured 2026-11-04)

55 synthetic pages with known truth (text, Hebrew text, table, sparse and blank pages; two creases, thirds,
cross, curl each way, wrinkles; 1240 x 1754, photo-like shading, noise and JPEG), the whole page as the
quad. Straightness is the SCAN-36 line metric (px, noise floor under 1); "FLAT" means the shipped fit
returned no bend on a bent page. Scripts and the corpus generator live in the session scratchpad.

| damage | what the shipped fit does |
|---|---|
| curl bowing the rows (curl_x, curl_big) | sees it on text and tables and straightens the rows (text 5.4 to 0.8 px, rules 6.8 to 0.3); it settles the rows at the bow's peak, so the page is straight but shifted (position recovered only 13-29%) |
| curl across the rows, horizontal creases, thirds_h | FLAT on text, Hebrew text, sparse and blank pages (page off by 20-43 px); a table's rules straighten through columns and edge curves |
| vertical creases (crease_v, thirds_v) | sees a 4-7 px bend on text but the kink stays (8.5 to 7.3 px) |
| two creases (cross) | sees a large bend and makes it worse: text 9.1 to 11.2 px, table rules 4.1 to 15.5 |
| wrinkles | the 3.6 px wrinkle is never seen; the 12 px one is seen on text (5.2 to 2.2 px), and on tables it adds error |
| sparse and blank pages | FLAT on everything |
| flat control | untouched, no harm |

- The edge-curve step is the one that hurts tables (curl_y 16% recovery without it, -30% with; wrinkle_strong
  -8% against -59%): it should not run unless the page edge is seen against a background.
- Recovery here is dense residual against the known truth (flow, rules or figure edges for the pages flow cannot
  read), so the finder's job is judged on position, not only on straight rows.

Acceptance addition: the finder beats these rows on the same corpus, and the corpus (generator only, no photos)
joins the repo as a fixture set once the finder is built.
