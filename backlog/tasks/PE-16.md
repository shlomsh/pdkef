# PE-16 · Folds in phone photos

*Opened 2026-11-04, shrunk the same day.* **Track: phone photo of a page** (not book scans; see the handoff's two
tracks). The first version of this ticket planned a fold finder that split a page into facets. Measured on
Shlomi's pages, that work has nothing to fix yet ([SCAN-19's follow-up](SCAN-19.md) has the evidence):

- His folds are faint lines with a kink of about 0.5 degree, one each on IMG_4757 and IMG_4758.
- The Document look already removes both: a dedicated shading repair changes only 0.53% and 0.31% of its pixels.
- `page-dewarp`, Leptonica, PP-OCRv6 tiny and M-LSD were run, and none improved a real photo.

## Outcome

The shipped fit never makes a folded page worse than the corners alone would.

## Acceptance

- [ ] On the synthetic corpus, the fit leaves a page no worse than it found it: today `cross` goes from 9.1 to 11.2
  px on text and from 4.1 to 15.5 on table rules.
- [ ] The edge-curve step does not run unless the page edge is seen against a background (it costs tables: curl_y
  16% recovery without it, -30% with; wrinkle_strong -8% against -59%).
- [ ] The control pages and the four photos are unchanged.

## What would reopen the larger work

A real photo where a fold visibly breaks text lines or rules in the Document look. The synthetic numbers say a
better row model can work (vertical crease 7.3 px shipped, 1.4 with Leptonica), so the idea to take then is the
model, not the packages: both crop or decline real pages. The other open item from a real page is the 5-7 px dent
in the boxed block's border at the IMG_4758 fold, one case so far. Fold shading in the Original look stays as it
is unless Shlomi asks for it.

## Baseline: the shipped fit on the synthetic corpus (measured 2026-11-04)

55 synthetic pages with known truth (text, Hebrew text, table, sparse and blank pages; two creases, thirds,
cross, curl each way, wrinkles; 1240 x 1754, photo-like shading, noise and JPEG), the whole page as the
quad. Straightness is the SCAN-36 line metric (px, noise floor under 1); "FLAT" means the shipped fit
returned no bend on a bent page. The creases are 3 degrees, about six times the real ones. Scripts and the corpus
generator live in the session scratchpad.

| damage | what the shipped fit does |
|---|---|
| curl bowing the rows (curl_x, curl_big) | sees it on text and tables and straightens the rows (text 5.4 to 0.8 px, rules 6.8 to 0.3); it settles the rows at the bow's peak, so the page is straight but shifted (position recovered only 13-29%) |
| curl across the rows, horizontal creases, thirds_h | FLAT on text, Hebrew text, sparse and blank pages (page off by 20-43 px); a table's rules straighten through columns and edge curves |
| vertical creases (crease_v, thirds_v) | sees a 4-7 px bend on text but the kink stays (8.5 to 7.3 px) |
| two creases (cross) | sees a large bend and makes it worse: text 9.1 to 11.2 px, table rules 4.1 to 15.5 |
| wrinkles | the 3.6 px wrinkle is never seen; the 12 px one is seen on text (5.2 to 2.2 px), and on tables it adds error |
| sparse and blank pages | FLAT on everything |
| flat control | untouched, no harm |
