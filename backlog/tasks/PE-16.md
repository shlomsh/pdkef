# PE-16 · Creases and wrinkles in phone photos

*Opened 2026-11-04.* **Track: phone photo of a page** (not book scans; see the handoff's two tracks). From
[SCAN-19](SCAN-19.md): on Shlomi's folded letters (IMG_4757, IMG_4758) the shipped dewarp finds no bend, and no
open-source package or permissive model does better (research 2026-11-04: every dewarper fits one smooth sheet
from long text rows; only Unfolder targets folds and has no code).

## Outcome

A sheet folded in halves or thirds is treated as facets: a fold finder (shading dip plus the kink of text rows
and rules across it) splits the corner-flattened page into pieces, each gets its own lean and bend (the book
result: one rotation per piece reaches the noise floor), and the pieces are joined in one resample. Wrinkles and
curl keep the smooth fit. Pages with no rows or rules are left to their corners.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Scored on the synthetic warped-page corpus (creases, thirds, cross, curl, wrinkles; text, table, sparse and
  blank pages) against the shipped fit: recovery per case is reported here and the finder beats the shipped fit on
  every crease case that has text or rules, without harming the control (identity) pages or the flat photos.
- [ ] IMG_4757 and IMG_4758: the kink across the fold is measured before and after (before: -0.5 and -2.3 deg).
- [ ] Pure function with synthetic unit tests; on a page with no detectable fold the plan is the plain page.
