# PE-03 · Fold finder for two pages to a sheet

*Opened 2026-11-04* from [the handoff](../../docs/scan-enhance-handoff.md), part of the page-enhancement plan.

## Outcome

A book spread's spine is found as a thin slanted dark line and the sheet splits into two pieces.

## Acceptance

- [ ] On the SCAN-36 book PDF's 8 pages the spine is found on 8/8 (tilt about 1.2 to 3.1 degrees), as in the prototype findings.
- [ ] A single page finds no fold.
- [ ] The finder is a pure function with a synthetic unit test.
