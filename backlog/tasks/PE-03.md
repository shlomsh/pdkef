# PE-03 · Fold finder for two pages to a sheet

*Opened 2026-11-04* from [the handoff](../../docs/scan-enhance-handoff.md), part of the page-enhancement plan.

## Outcome

A book spread's spine is found as a thin slanted dark line and the sheet splits into two pieces. **Each piece is then a page of its own** (Shlomi, 2026-11-04: academic scans often squeeze two book pages onto one landscape sheet, and the better approach is to handle each page separately, as if they were different pages): its own lean, its own bend, its own squeeze, never one fit across both. The split is the first step of `planPage`, before anything is measured; every later step sees two ordinary pages.

## Acceptance

- [ ] On the SCAN-36 book PDF's 8 pages the spine is found on 8/8 (tilt about 1.2 to 3.1 degrees), as in the prototype findings.
- [ ] A single page finds no fold.
- [ ] The finder is a pure function with a synthetic unit test.
- [ ] Nothing downstream measures across the spine: a test feeds a two-page sheet whose halves lean differently (the book's right halves lean -2.4 to -4.7 degrees, the left -1.4 to +0.3) and each half is straightened with its own lean.
