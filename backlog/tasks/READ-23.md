# READ-23 · Every page fills the width, and notes keep one column

*Filed 2026-11-05 from Shlomi's screenshots of a scanned article: two portrait pages, then a landscape spread.*

Every page was drawn at one zoom, so a portrait page next to a landscape spread was narrower, and the
notes column took all the room the page left: beside a portrait page a note was far wider than beside the
spread, and the screen beside the page went unused.

## Outcome

Every page of a document is as wide as its widest page, in its own shape, so a page and its notes fill
the width on every page. Notes keep one width and one place all the way down. The zoom percent is the
widest page's. This holds in the normal view, the lamp and on a phone; print and the saved file keep each
page's own size.
