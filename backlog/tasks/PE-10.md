# PE-10 · Cleanup inside the text column

*Opened 2026-11-04* from [the handoff](../../docs/scan-enhance-handoff.md), part of the page-enhancement plan. **Track: flatbed or copier scan of a book** (not phone photos; see the handoff's two tracks).

## Outcome

Cleanup inside the justified text column removes the fold line and dark edges.

**Where the black stain comes from** (Shlomi, 2026-11-04): the book is scanned lying open, spine up, on the glass, so the gutter and the outer page edges lift off the glass and scan as black bands and streaks along the margins (the dark gutter on the book PDF). The stain is therefore a band along the spine and the outer edges, wider where the page lifts most, and it is cleaned per page after the spread is split (PE-03), each half on its own.

**Prototype, 2026-11-04, outside the app:** removing, outside the text block only, what touches the page border, what runs long and thin, stacked thin fragments, and on the spine side any mark holding a dark vertical run of 40 px or more. All 16 pages kept their text block pixel for pixel, the sampled hand marks survived (circled 3, "2213", arrow, underlines), and 1.7 to 21% of a page's ink went. Small specks remain in the margins.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Marginalia: keep or remove follows Shlomi's answer to the open question in [the handoff](../../docs/scan-enhance-handoff.md) (the cleanup removed a handwritten "2213" and a stamp).
- [ ] The fold line, the dark gutter and the dark edge bands are gone on all 16 halves of the book PDF, scored with the SCAN-36 cleanup measures: in-column ink kept, ink and components left outside the column, large leftover streaks and blobs (target: none over a set size).
- [ ] A stain that touches text (a band running into the first or last letters of a line) keeps every glyph; never trade text for a clean edge.
- [ ] Cleanup is off for pages where it cannot tell text column from margin.
