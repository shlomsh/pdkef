# AI-04 · Try four PDF forms and measure accuracy and correction effort

## Why

Learn whether AI saves effort on old PDF forms while separating missed fields, false fields and wrong x/y placement.

## Scope and acceptance

- [ ] Try four non-personal English/Hebrew × scanned/flat digital forms, using synthetic facts and independently marked expected fields.
- [ ] Record recall/precision with existing one-to-one IoU≥0.5 scoring, labels, invented/missing answers and actual exported positions; undefined scores stay undefined.
- [ ] Record manual corrections and time to usable PDF versus manual filling on those samples; pin model/settings and describe limitations.
- [ ] Fix invented facts, wrong-page positions, uneditable output, RTL/export failures, stale overwrites or lost edits before beta.
- [ ] Browser-check complete flow plus cancellation/offline/limited-access manual fallback; narrow public claims to demonstrated support.

## Notes

Small learning trial, not a large certification programme. 90/90/85 is a useful prior target rather than a broad-corpus prerequisite. Do not infer universal accuracy from four forms.
