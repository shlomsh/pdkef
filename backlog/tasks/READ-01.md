# READ-01 · Clickable sketches of the mark popup and the fullscreen bar

*Filed 2026-11-01.* Design record: [docs/highlight-pdf.md](../../docs/highlight-pdf.md). Written pitches
came first; this is the sketch step, before any production code.

## Outcome

Shlomi clicks through two or three sketches of each piece on a real page (a scanned Hebrew paper and an
English one) and picks one of each:

- **The mark popup** at the end of a band: six colors, the underline style, Comment; Delete on a selected
  mark. Nothing else.
- **The fullscreen bar**: one line, 32 to 36 px icon buttons with tooltips on a fine pointer, the file name
  and "Page X of N" inline, no card chrome; 44 px targets kept on coarse pointers.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Sketches built in the scratchpad or a prototype route, not in `src/tools/sign/`.
- [ ] Each sketch checked at three desktop widths and on an iPad width before handover.
- [ ] Shlomi's pick recorded in the design record.
