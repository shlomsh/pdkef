# READ-27 · Feedback moves into Highlight's tool row

*Filed 2026-11-06.* Shlomi asked for Highlight's Feedback to sit where Sign's does. The first attempt stayed uncommitted
in the main checkout from 2026-11-05 and was written for a six-tile row, before Share made it seven, so it never shipped.

Feedback leaves the end of the file line and becomes a tile after Redo, Sign's position, icon-only above the phone. Eight
tiles do not fit one phone row at the 44 px floor, so at 480 px and below it stands down, as Sign's does at a narrower
width, and the phone row stays as it was. It stays out of the lamp.
