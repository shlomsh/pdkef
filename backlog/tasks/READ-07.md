# READ-07 · Highlight without arming

*Filed 2026-11-01.* Design record: [docs/highlight-pdf.md](../../docs/highlight-pdf.md), Open calls 2 and 3.
Zotero's locked mode is the nearest model.

## Outcome

On desktop, dragging along a line in Highlight PDF lays a band with nothing armed; Undo is the safety net.
On a phone, a one-finger swipe still scrolls and a long-press then drag highlights, with no arming and no
Stop chip. Sign is unchanged.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Shlomi's decision on the one-shot rule recorded in CLAUDE.md's invariant, scoped to this tool.
- [ ] On a real iPhone, long-press then drag on a digital PDF does not open the browser's text selection.
