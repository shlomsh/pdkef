# PE-11 · Unsqueeze toward the spine

*Opened 2026-11-04* from [the handoff](../../docs/scan-enhance-handoff.md), part of the page-enhancement plan.

## Outcome

Right pages that narrow 4 to 10% toward the spine are widened horizontally, each page on its own (the left page narrows toward the spine on its other side, mirrored).

## Acceptance

- [ ] 5 of 8 book pages measure narrower; after unsqueeze they match the paper aspect within 3%.
- [ ] Horizontal only; no new vertical stretch.
