# PE-11 · Found corners square a book page

*Opened 2026-11-04* from [the handoff](../../docs/scan-enhance-handoff.md), part of the page-enhancement plan. **Track: flatbed or copier scan of a book** (not phone photos; see the handoff's two tracks).

**Route, decided by Shlomi 2026-11-04:** a crooked PDF is straightened in Scan and comes back to Highlight, not rebuilt inside Highlight. Squaring a page is not an affine move, so the vector rebuild this plan first had could not do it.

## Outcome

`planPage` gives each book page corners that square its text block, instead of a level rectangle. They reach out to the page's margins, so handwriting stays in the page. Scan's corners then do the rest, with no new warp.

**Measured on the book, 2026-11-04.** 15 of 16 pages have a text block 3 to 7% wider at one end, with the edge beside the spine slanting and the outer edge nearly straight. Line spacing drifts too, 4.2% on average top to bottom, so this is tilt and not only a sideways squeeze: a four-corner warp brings that to 2.1%, where stretching rows alone (this ticket's first plan) would leave it. Page 143 has four lines, too few to fit.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Each edge is fitted to the justified rows only: short last lines and indents do not move it, and the fit is deterministic.
- [ ] After the warp, the top and bottom widths of the text block agree within 1% on the 15 pages.
- [ ] Line spacing drift stays at or under 2.5% on average.
- [ ] A page with too few rows keeps the level rectangle.
- [ ] Corners stay inside the sheet, and the hand marks in the margin stay inside the corners.
