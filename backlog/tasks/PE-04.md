# PE-04 · Lean as a rotation before the bend

*Opened 2026-11-04* from [the handoff](../../docs/scan-enhance-handoff.md), part of the page-enhancement plan. **Track: flatbed or copier scan of a book** (not phone photos; see the handoff's two tracks).

## Outcome

Lean is a quad rotation applied before the bend is measured, reusing `rasterInk.estimateSkewDegrees` (range up to 6 degrees).

## Acceptance

- [x] A half of a spread is levelled by turning its corners, so nothing leans into `measureBend`. Its row shear stays for phone photos, whose rows the corners already set: the fit after the lean changes nothing on a book half (2.03 to 2.01 px), and a book half skips it (PE-05).
- [x] `readInkBounds` treated any rotation as sideways: confirmed, and an upside-down page is now read (a test). Only 90 and 270 are sideways. Print keeps its own check.
- [x] Lean is estimated per piece, so the two halves of a spread get different rotations (PE-03 splits first).
- [x] Rotating each half by its lean leaves rows within 1 to 2 px on the book pages: median 1.98 px, worst 2.28 px, where the noise floor is about 2 (PE-03 has the run).

Measured 2026-11-04 (PE-03 has the table): the lean rotation reaches the noise floor on every half of the book
(median 2.03, worst 2.24 px), and the shipped bend fit afterwards changes nothing and can misfire through its edge
curves, so for a book half the plan is lean, then optionally per-line straightening, with edge curves off.
