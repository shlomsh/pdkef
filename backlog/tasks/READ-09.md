# READ-09 · A pen, iPad first

*Filed 2026-11-01.* Design record: [docs/highlight-pdf.md](../../docs/highlight-pdf.md). "annotate pdf ipad"
was the largest device query in the 2026-11-01 Trends read; with a mouse almost nobody draws.

## Outcome

An armed Pen in Highlight PDF draws ink with a pencil or a finger on iPad, and with a mouse on desktop, in
the six highlight colors. Strokes export as standard `/Ink` annotations and read back as editable strokes.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Checked with an Apple Pencil on a real iPad: palm rejection, no scroll while drawing.
- [ ] Export then read-back of ink is lossless.
