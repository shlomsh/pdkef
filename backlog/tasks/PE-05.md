# PE-05 · assess and planPage

*Opened 2026-11-04* from [the handoff](../../docs/scan-enhance-handoff.md), part of the page-enhancement plan.

## Outcome

`assess(gray)` returns an `Assessment` of turn, turnConfidence, leanDeg, spread, bentPx and crooked; `planPage` builds a `PagePlan { pieces }` from it. Both types start here (PE-02 made `Piece`).

## Acceptance

- [ ] [unverified] One-bit pages area-averaged at 1000 px may lose rows: measure on a one-bit fixture and fix or document.
- [ ] 90 versus 270 degrees: the default and the Rotate fallback follow Shlomi's answer to the open question in [the handoff](../../docs/scan-enhance-handoff.md).
- [ ] The page's quarter turn is known before the bend is measured; output is sized with `warpSizeFor(pageAspect)`.
- [ ] Shlomi's photos come out upright and at 1.41 within 3%.
